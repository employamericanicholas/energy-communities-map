Qualifying FFE energy community MSA / non-MSA (not qualifying)

Regions are dissolved from the exact Treasury/IRS county groupings. Solid outlines are Metropolitan Statistical Areas (MSAs); dashed outlines are non-MSA (nonmetropolitan) areas. Green-filled regions are the MSAs/non-MSAs that qualify as Fossil Fuel Employment energy communities for that vintage (they meet the FFE threshold and the unemployment requirement). The qualifying determination is made at the MSA/non-MSA level, so every county in a green area qualifies. When a vintage is selected, county lines are shown; click any county to see its Vintage 1 and Vintage 2 MSA/non-MSA.

Sources & notes

Eligibility data: IRS Notice 2026-39 (and cumulative prior notices 2023-29, 2023-47, 2024-30/48, 2025-31). Coal eligibility is at the census-tract level; FFE at the county level.

Statistical Area (FFE) eligibility reflects the 2025 calendar-year unemployment rates in Notice 2026-39 and is applicable as of June 10, 2026, until the IRS issues its next (2027) update. Coal closure tracts are cumulative through Notice 2026-39.

Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary files (counties & CBSAs 2020 and 2023). Nuclear sites: Wikipedia/DBpedia (operating & former commercial power reactors; research, military, and never-built sites excluded), plus NRC Early Site Permit (ESP), Combined License (COL), and advanced-reactor sites from NRC records.
